What is Vedic Pran Pratishtha and why is it essential? +
Pran Pratishtha is the sacred Vedic consecration ceremony through which an inert mineral or seed is spiritually infused with cosmic life force (Prana). In our Varanasi and Haridwar sanctums, certified priests bathe each item in Gangajal, Panchamrit, and offer dhoop while chanting ancient planetary beeja mantras. This ensures your crystal or Rudraksha arrives pulsating with pure divine vibration rather than factory dust.
How do I know my Rudraksha or Gemstone is genuine? +
Every piece is verified in government-recognized ISO 9001:2015 gemological laboratories. We include the physical laboratory report with microscopic magnification tests, specific gravity, refractive index, and a scannable QR code linking to the lab's online database.
Can women wear Rudraksha? Are there any menstrual restrictions? +
Yes, absolutely! Classical Vedic scriptures like the Shiva Purana and Srimad Devi Bhagavatam explicitly state that Rudraksha can and should be worn by everyone regardless of gender, caste, or age. Shiva's grace is universal. While some choose to take off beads during child-delivery or death ceremonies (Sutak period), daily wear is fully auspicious.
How often should I cleanse my crystal bracelets? +
Crystals absorb ambient energetic vibrations. Cleanse your bracelets once every 30 days under the gentle light of the Full Moon (Purnima) for 3 to 4 hours. You can also place them on a Selenite charging plate or pass them through sacred White Sage / Guggul smoke. Avoid submerging soft stones (like Selenite or Pyrite) in water.
